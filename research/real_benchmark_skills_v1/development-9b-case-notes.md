# Preliminary 9B development cases

These are official SkillsBench v1.1 **development** observations under the frozen configuration. Four of eight 9B task pairs are complete, with one additional S0 cell awaiting its S1 pair; no held-out result or overall 9B treatment estimate is available yet. Original task verifiers determine binary resolution. Fractional reward and subtests explain failures but do not override the official outcome. Full trajectories, artifacts and requests remain private, with separate local backup.

## `dialogue-parser`, S0 No Skill

Its six top-level CTRF categories had one pass and five failures; the detailed pytest run passed 15 of 35 parameterized checks.
