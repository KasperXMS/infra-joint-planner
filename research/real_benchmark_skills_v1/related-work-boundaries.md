# Related-work boundaries before development outcomes

These notes identify existing directions that a later S2 mechanism must distinguish itself from. They do not establish novelty, reproduce any paper's result, or substitute published scores for this study's missing model outcomes. Sources were checked on 2026-10-13. Method details below were checked in available primary HTML. For AWM, the detailed method is in the authors' arXiv v1 HTML, alongside its PMLR proceedings entry.

| Work | Existing contribution relevant to this study | Consequence for a candidate S2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SkillsBench, v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670v4) | Paired native-skill evaluation with deterministic task verifiers; skill gains vary across configurations, and smaller models with skills can sometimes match larger models without them. | A positive native-skill lift or a small/large comparison alone is replication evidence. The contribution would need a supported failure mechanism and new controlled evidence. |
| [Presentation granularity study](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31408) | Controls skill abstraction and worked examples on 30 oracle-validated tasks, with five repeated trials per condition for GPT-5.5 and DeepSeek V4-Flash. It separates skill availability from presentation effects and uses tasks as the inference unit. | Making instructions more concrete or adding examples is already studied. The current 4B/9B, non-thinking, one-trial design is a different scope, with substantially weaker repeatability evidence. |
| [SkillLens, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08386) | Organizes policies, strategies, procedures and primitives in a graph; retrieves mixed-granularity units and routes them through accept/decompose/rewrite/skip decisions. It evolves both procedural and routing knowledge. | Hierarchical selection, state-dependent local adaptation, and compact task-specific contexts require a specific distinction, not a new label. Account for the additional router/verifier decisions and their cost. |
| [SkillReducer](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29919) | Optimizes routing descriptions and restructures skill bodies into actionable core content plus supplementary material available through progressive disclosure, with faithfulness checks. | Compression or deferred reference loading alone is insufficient. Preserve expertise and report token/content changes so a success effect is not attributed solely to wording. |
| [SkillCraft, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00718) | Benchmarks acquisition and reuse of higher-level tool compositions across compositional scenarios, rather than only instance-level atomic-tool success. | Executable tool composition and reuse are existing questions. Do not replace this study's official SkillsBench tasks with a new synthetic composition benchmark. |
| [SkillSmith, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15215v1) | Compiles skill packages into boundary contracts with operational interfaces, source provenance, policies and fallback paths. Its runtime selectively exposes and executes compiled content; compile and solve costs are separated. | Typed executable interfaces, source-grounded fallback and resumable compiled workflows are existing mechanisms. A prospective S2 must state what differs and count offline construction cost and any stronger-model contribution. |
| [SkVM, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.03088v3) | Profiles model/harness capabilities, applies compensation or substitution, binds environment dependencies, extracts concurrency, and uses runtime adaptation/code solidification. | Capability-based rewriting, absolute-path repair, environment preparation and delegation are covered directions. This study does not launch a separate capability/backend benchmark or impose a synthetic DAG on official tasks. |
| [Agent Workflow Memory](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/wang25bx.html), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.07429) | Stores abstract subroutines as goal descriptions plus state, reasoning and executable action steps. Its offline mode induces from training examples; online mode admits workflows from trajectories judged successful by an LM evaluator. It also tests exposing workflows as callable actions: the agent calls them in only 18.5% of tasks, and fixed action sequences can fail when intermediate states change. | Workflow induction, executable shortcuts and low action uptake are already studied. A prospective S2 must distinguish its delivery and evidence, avoid learning from held-out trajectories, account for any evaluator or stronger model, and retain all construction/inference costs. |

The current defensible scope is an official-task reproduction and diagnostic study of 4B/9B native skill consumption under fixed inference and harness settings. RQ9 remains open until actual development failures support a mechanism that can be compared fairly with the nearest methods. Omitting S2 is acceptable if no such mechanism survives limited development. No outcome-driven task selection, stronger-model rescue, or unreported schema constraints are permitted.
