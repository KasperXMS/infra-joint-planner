# Method novelty collision audit v1

Date: 2026-10-04. Branch: cost-guidance-exploration-v1.
Status: literature-grounded design audit, **not an experimental method result**.
The [literature map](method-literature-map-v1.md) records all21 requested
original-body readings, provenance, authors, venues and version/visual limitations.
Findings below concern those read versions, not an exhaustive priority search.

## Bottom line

The broad story already overlaps heavily with existing research. We cannot
claim to introduce cost-aware agent workflows, online semantic graph changes,
infrastructure-aware MAS, or logical/physical separation. A defensible candidate
is narrower: test a verifiable, action-specific consequence interface between
an execution-grown semantic Agent and an unchanged physical scheduler, under
distributed artifact locality and heterogeneous service costs. That conjunction
is **not yet demonstrated here** and its absence elsewhere is not proven.

The completed Raw-Aware experiment is motivation, not a validated method.
It contains a quality-preserving Academic Slow pair, costly quality gains,
cheap wrong answers and stopping expansion. It does not establish causal
superiority, a placement reversal, or universal insufficiency of raw profiles.

## Closest collisions, without straw-man comparisons

### EnumGRPO: strongest semantic formulation collision

[Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.03152v1), sections2-3.
Its Agent already constructs steps from intermediate relations and optimizes
semantic granularity/context/ordering. Offline enumeration distills quality-cost
experience into prompt heuristics. It is not merely a fixed SQL plan search.
Its placement axis is semantic ordering, not measured device/link placement.
Our proposed difference is distributed runtime consequences through a separate
physical service, not the notion of interleaved planning. RouteE has especially
high collision risk; copying its distillation and renaming it would not suffice.

### Abacus: strongest cost/quality estimator collision

[Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14661v3), sections2-4.
Cost/latency/quality estimation, implementation choice, Pareto optimization and
sampling are established. Its label/judge-based quality model and optimizer
sampling cannot silently become our quality oracle or a no-execution quote.
Our empirical service buckets are modest infrastructure estimates, not a new
general semantic-operator optimizer. Unknown support must remain unknown.

### INFRAMIND: direct infra-aware MAS collision

[Original](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11440v1), sections3-6.
Load/cache/deadline feedback and joint topology/role/model policies overlap.
Do not call its whole planner finite just because its executor has fifteen
model-strategy combinations. The read design commits initial topology and places
runtime topology revision in future work. A possible distinction is continuous
evidence-dependent artifact/reduction evolution, not generic infrastructure
awareness or quality-constrained orchestration.

### Murakkab: strongest systems/architecture collision

[Read preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18298v2),
[published venue](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/presentation/chaudhry).
Declarative separation, heterogeneous profiles, SLOs and adaptive execution
already exist. Configuration knobs can change tasks/frame counts/debate;
calling it physical-only would be wrong. Evidence-conditioned open-ended
tool decisions and no-execution action quotes are not established in the read
version. The published-version check below now closes the preprint-only limitation;
no strong absence claim follows from a version comparison alone.

### Flow/AFlow and LLMCompiler: graph evolution/search collision

[Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07834),
[AFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10762),
[LLMCompiler](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/kim24y.html).
Flow changes tasks and dependencies at runtime. AFlow searches code workflows
using actual validation executions; it is primarily offline. LLMCompiler
supports iterative replanning and parallel function dispatch. Their omission
of our distributed quote interface is not evidence that dynamic graphs are new.
Their role is semantic-control lineage/comparison, not automatic replacements
for the current SDK-native Manager.

### DynTaskMAS and heterogeneous-GPU orchestration: important adjacent overlap

[DynTaskMAS](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/36130),
[heterogeneous-GPU work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03335).
DynTaskMAS already combines changing graphs with resource/context-transfer
feedback; its adaptive allocation estimator is underspecified in the read paper.
The GPU work can schedule an incrementally exposed logical graph, not only an
upfront frozen DAG. It preserves logical semantics/bindings while optimizing
physical realization. We must demonstrate semantic decisions beyond what the
shared scheduler already optimizes, rather than weaken these comparators.

### Adaptive Query Processing: lineage, not a novelty shortcut

[Complete author original](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~amol/papers/fnt-aqp.pdf),
chapters3 and6-9. Runtime feedback, intermediate reuse, remote delays and
monitor/analyze/plan/actuate overhead are established. Some mechanisms introduce
new operators when sources stall. Their relational correctness assumptions do
not apply to LLM evidence reduction. Quote-before-Commit is a candidate interface
experiment, not an optimal policy theorem inherited from AQP.

## Candidate difference: conjunction and evidence burden

| Dimension | Already covered individually | What we would need to demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dynamic heterogeneous infrastructure | Infra systems and AQP | Quotes change with genuine current profiles, not only renamed static costs |
| Distributed artifact locality | Distributed query/physical execution lineage | Artifact readiness/replicas affect movement consequences without identity leakage |
| Open-ended semantic workflow | EnumGRPO/Flow/LLMCompiler/DynTaskMAS | Same finite primitives, no predefined path pool or hand-written workflow |
| Online execution-grown planning | Existing Agent and AQP work | Real observations guide next actions while completed work remains immutable |
| Logical/physical separation | Existing serving/compiler work | Semantic Agent cannot choose device/deployment; shared scheduler resolves it |
| Action-level consequence feedback | Operator cost models/economic tool use overlap | Auditable non-executing quote, uncertainty, commit/revision, prediction-versus-actual |

No single read work has been shown to instantiate this entire conjunction in
the same experimental setting. This is a **bounded coverage observation**, not
a first-ever claim. A mere concatenation of known components is insufficient:
the method needs evidence that the interface produces useful semantic changes,
with quality and overhead reported across workloads.

## What would count against the proposed mechanism

- Ledger matches Quote across workloads: simpler accounting may be sufficient;
  Quote loses its justification even if technically implemented.
- Quotes are mostly unknown or inaccurate: the interface cannot support its
  claimed decisions; investigate estimation support without inventing values.
- Manager ignores correct quotes: distinguish information from decision policy;
  do not relabel this method behavior a runtime bug.
- Fewer bytes but wrong answers: no quality-preserving optimization claim.
- Added reasoning costs erase savings: reject or narrow the route.
- Only one task improves or requires a task recipe: no reusable mechanism claim.
- The published closest work implements the same online interface and setting:
  revisit novelty rather than defend the current storyline.

## Baseline and attribution decisions

Keep current native Blind and Raw-Aware clean traces as historical references.
Ledger is an attributed Budget-Aware-inspired baseline, not BATS reproduction:
no restarts, new verifier, summary or tier-dependent strategy hints. Quote uses
cost-model/AQP lineage but must not execute samples as Abacus/Palimpzest do.
Trace heuristics, if tried, stay separately versioned and explicitly EnumGRPO-
inspired. RouteLLM/FrugalGPT are model-routing lineage, not permission to add
model substitution or correctness prediction. Meta-tools motivates overhead
accounting, not a vocabulary expansion in this exploration.

## Historical pre-experiment disposition

Broad novelty collisions are real. They do not yet prove that the full narrow
method is already covered, so the authorized literature-collision stop condition
is **not established**. Proceed to a small, falsifiable Ledger/Quote prototype
comparison. Do not declare a primary method or novel contribution before actual
cross-workload tests. Complete the closest published-version comparison before
final recommendation; retain unresolved evidence as unresolved.

## Final published-version and experimental disposition

The [published Murakkab paper](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi26-chaudhry.pdf)
is now read. Dynamic request compositions and test-driven coding loops strengthen
the collision with broad workflow-evolution claims. Its profiled configuration/
MILP mechanism is not demonstrated equivalent to ready-action quoting. Full22-page
reading and Figure5/Table1 verification are recorded in the literature map.

The16-cell experiment does not establish useful semantic adaptation from mandatory
quotes. Narrow-interface novelty cannot substitute for quality/overhead benefit.
No primary method is promoted. [Final recommendation](next-method-recommendation-v1.md)
keeps Ledger as baseline and optional consequences as an untested next hypothesis.
