# Future fixed-workflow feasibility contracts

These are controlled future references, not a prescribed topology pool for an open-ended Planner. P1 executed only bounded operator probes; no full workflow quality or cost comparison was run.

| Contract | Information processing and dependencies | Required capabilities | Cost categories and quality risk | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W0 — centralized evidence processing | Source owners transfer full original text/table candidate artifacts or declared complete corpus shards to central processing; central retrieval/reasoning follows. Full acquisition, indexing and cache installation are charged as cold setup or per-query transfer according to actual reuse. | Versioned source reads/pulls, central retrieval/model with text/table contract, original evaluator. | Largest communication/setup and central context demand; quality depends on central model, not assumed equal to W1. | Transport executable; complete task answering untested. |
| W1 — distributed retrieval + global synthesis | Each publisher/source service runs local BM25 or permitted table access, transfers selected evidence with original IDs and scores, then a global component synthesizes. Its reduced representation is explicitly a retrieved passage/record set. | Source-local BM25/read, measured artifact pull, global model, evaluator. | Lower communication can omit needed evidence; top-k and distributed retrieval quality must be evaluated. | Local retrieval and cross-node transfer executable; global synthesis untested. |
| W2 — local semantic extraction + global synthesis | Local Agents would invoke supported models and transfer model-generated structured evidence before global reasoning. | Verified resident model modality, semantic extractor, provenance and answer-preservation checks. | Additional model-service latency/cost; summaries may lose decisive facts. | **Not implemented**: no verified text/table/image model deployment in this P1 environment. |

W0 and W1 differ in where retrieval/selection occurs, not merely which host receives the same bytes. Their output contract is the original benchmark answer format, and both require independent native scoring. For MultiHop publisher-comparison and MultiModalQA text+table tasks they are technically distinct; the D0 controls should be allowed to use one source. No workflow is called quality-acceptable until it actually answers and passes the original evaluator. Opportunity Coverage and Infrastructure Decision Sensitivity are defined over such observed quality-acceptable alternatives, and neither is reported from the static metadata here.
