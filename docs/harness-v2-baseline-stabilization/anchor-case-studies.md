# Anchor case studies

All observations below refer to the unchanged Stage 2 H1 task IDs: MultiHop-RAG `multihop-rag-train-9bae0079038050a37a1ae583`, LongBench-v2 `66f7c780bb02136c067c35e8`, and Video-MME `video_mme:795`. Per-cell action, artifact-flow, placement, cost and score records are in the [structural export](../../results/harness-v2-baseline-stabilization/workflow-variation.json); private answer text is intentionally absent.

## MultiHop-RAG

**Trace-supported observation.** All eight completed cells score 0; four incomplete cells are Verifier protocol failures. Blind slow trial 1 reached 12 retrievals and two model actions before a schema failure. Ledger slow trials 1/2 each stopped after six retrievals and one *logical* model action; no physical inference or nonempty Manager model result had been materialized at stop. Ledger fast trials used 18 retrievals and three model actions and completed, but both answers scored 0. Aware fast trial 2 added `select_fields`/`top_k_records`; it also scored 0. This rules out simple “more retrievals equals task success” and identifies repeated retrieval/evidence consumption as a useful next diagnostic target. The redacted trace shows what artifacts were passed to models, but cannot certify that the required multi-hop evidence was retrieved or faithfully synthesized. No hidden gold answer was used to relabel intermediate candidates, and an alleged “correct answer later rewritten” is **not verified**.

## LongBench-v2

**Trace-supported observation.** Of 12 cells, only Slow Ledger trial 1 and Fast Aware trial 2 completed; both scored 0. Eight are raw Verifier schema failures, one is the separate Manager turn-budget exhaustion, and one is the terminal choice-contract mismatch. Aware used 12 `bm25_retrieve` and three `invoke_model` actions in each of its four cells, showing unchanged coarse operators under slow/fast while outcomes still vary. Blind additionally used `aggregate_artifacts` in several cells; that changed evidence handling but did not yield a correct result. Fast Blind trial 2 settled 65 semantic action attempts and exhausted the 20-turn Manager budget, a genuine bounded-run outcome. The frozen physical preflight made over-context attempts explicit; there is no evidence of silent truncation. Schema failures and terminal format errors blocked evaluation of some nonempty candidate outputs, but their correctness is unknown. Neither “Verifier alone caused low TSR” nor “retrieval coverage was sufficient” follows from these traces.

## Video-MME

**Trace-supported observation.** All 12 video cells completed. Blind scored 3/4, Aware 2/4, Ledger 1/4 across networks/trials. Every cell began with `sample_frames`; Slow Aware trial 1 also read an artifact and extracted a clip, then scored 0 while Blind scored 1. Fast Ledger trial 2 generated a contact sheet and scored 1, whereas its first trial scored 0. Blind/aware success swaps across fast trials: Blind 1/0 and Aware 0/1. Slow Video trial 2 is the only paired score-1 Aware/Blind cost improvement, with Aware using fewer frames in its second model call; the paired first trial fails quality preservation. These traces show frame selection and repeated model use can affect evidence flow, but not a repeatable infrastructure response. Approximately 282 MB of source video moved in each cell, making network bandwidth the dominant slow-network E2E factor independently of the logical plan.

These anchors expose different failure modes: MultiHop has completed wrong answers and protocol-censored paths; LongBench is largely protocol/budget-censored with explicit context feasibility limits; Video completes but answer quality and frame paths vary between samples. The three tasks are too few to infer general TSR significance.
